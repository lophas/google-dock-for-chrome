Google Dock

Your Google apps, one gesture away.

v0.4.0

Appearance & behavior

A taller zone reveals the dock before your pointer reaches the macOS Dock activation edge. It does not block page clicks.

Apps in your dock

Check apps to pin them. Drag rows or use the arrows to change their order.

Changes save automatically

The dock appears on normal HTTP/HTTPS websites. Chrome internal pages, the Chrome Web Store and the built-in PDF viewer are restricted; use the toolbar launcher there. After loading or reloading the extension, refresh existing website tabs.
