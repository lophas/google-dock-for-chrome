Google Dock

Your Google apps, one gesture away.

v1.0.4

Appearance & behavior

A taller zone reveals the dock before your pointer reaches the macOS Dock activation edge. It does not block page clicks.

0% is opaque; 100% removes the background tint and blur. App icons stay fully visible.

Apps in your dock

Check apps to pin them. Drag rows or use the arrows to change their order.

Google accounts per app

Use a different signed-in Google account for an app. Open the app, select your preferred account there, then return here and remember its last-used tab. This does not change your Chrome profile.

Backup & restore

Save your pinned apps and their order to a JSON file. Restoring replaces the pinned list; appearance and behavior settings stay as they are.

Changes save automatically

The dock appears on normal HTTP/HTTPS websites. Chrome internal pages, the Chrome Web Store and the built-in PDF viewer are restricted; use the toolbar launcher there. After loading or reloading the extension, refresh existing website tabs.
